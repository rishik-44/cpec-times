[HEADING: Celebrating Hispanic Heritage at CPEC]
by Carlos Aguilar [BR] [BR]

Hispanic Heritage Month is celebrated from September 15th to October 15th, and at CPEC, that means celebrating our rich cultural heritage and diversity in many ways. One of the ways we celebrate Hispanic heritage is our annual homeroom door decorating contest. This year is our fourth year holding this competition, and it is great to our students collaborating with each other in decorating our doors, making CPEC more vibrant and colorful while celebrating Hispanic heritage and continuing school traditions,

[BR]
But that’s not all. Along with our door competition, we have our upcoming Hispanic Heritage Spirit Week as well. This spirit week allows students to express their cultures and traditions while also representing their countries and diverse ethnic backgrounds. Spirit week is a great opportunity for our students to share their cultures and connect with others, so we hope to see everybody participating in this celebration of Hispanic heritage!

[HEADING: Is Cristiano Ronaldo's Portuguese Career Nearing Its End?]
By Rishik [BR][BR]


For more than two decades, Cristiano Ronaldo has been one of the biggest names in international soccer. But after a dramatic disagreement with Portugal's coach, Jorge Jesus, questions about the legendary forward's future with the national team have returned.[BR][BR]
Ronaldo, now 41, left Portugal's training camp in September after he was left on the bench against Norway and learned he would not start against Denmark. The disagreement sparked uncertainty about his relationship with the coaching staff and whether his time representing Portugal could be approaching its end.[BR][BR]
However, Ronaldo has not announced his international retirement. In a statement released in October, he apologized to fans and teammates for how he left camp while making clear that he remained available to represent Portugal. The Portuguese Football Federation later suspended him for 30 days following the incident.[BR][BR]
Despite the controversy, Ronaldo's legacy with Portugal remains remarkable. He has scored a record 146 international goals and helped his country win the 2016 European Championship and the UEFA Nations League twice. His experience and leadership have made him a major figure in Portuguese soccer for years.[BR][BR]
For now, the question is not whether Ronaldo has officially retired, but what comes next. Will he return to Portugal's lineup after his suspension, or is the national team beginning to move toward a new era? His next chapter remains uncertain, leaving fans waiting to see how the story unfolds.[BR][BR]
