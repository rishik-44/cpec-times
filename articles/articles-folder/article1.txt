According to an article from Yahoo! Sports written by Bryce Lazenby, the Panthers are expected to regress.[BR][BR]

Also, outside of wide receiver Tetairoa McMillan, there isn’t much offensive support for Young. Young played three 
seasons at Alabama before getting drafted by the Panthers with the top overall pick in 2023. While at Alabama, he 
was used to having an offensive line and receivers he could rely on. But he’s not getting this with the Panthers. 
In his first season with Carolina, he got sacked 62 times, which is more than he did in both seasons he started 
with Alabama combined. [BR][BR]

The outlook this season for the Panthers this year definitely isn’t great. Currently, the Buccaneers are the favorite 
to win the NFC South (Lazenby 2026). The Panthers have the third best odds (they are above the Falcons, but below the 
New Orleans Saints), but the Falcons could definitely give the Panthers, Saints, and the Buccaneers a run for their money. [BR]
